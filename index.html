Getting the Most Out of Cloud Hybrid AI

                    
Learn how to connect your favorite model providers, use voice prompts, customize themes, and manage local conversations.

                


                

                    
                    

                        

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            
1. Quick Start & Adding Keys

                            
Open Settings → API Management. Choose from 10 frontier providers (Groq, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, DeepSeek, etc.). Click Get Key to generate your key, paste it into the form, and click Create.

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            
2. Model Switcher

                            
Click the ⇄ switcher icon in the chat input bar at any time to instantly switch between active models without losing your conversation history.

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            
3. Voice Input (Speech-to-Text)

                            
Click the microphone button in the input bar to speak naturally. Cloud AI transcribes your voice in real time into the prompt area for instant hands-free chatting.

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            
4. Audio Read Aloud (TTS)

                            
Every response generated by the assistant features a speaker icon in the bottom-right action bar. Click it to listen to the answer read aloud.

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            
5. Local Disk Privacy

                            
Under Settings → Local Conversation Storage, choose any directory on your computer to persist encrypted conversation logs safely on your disk.

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                        

                            
                        

                        

                            
6. Themes & Wallpapers

                            
Head to Settings → Themes to pick from curated glass colorways (Purple, Green, Orange, Blue, Moon) or upload your own custom photo wallpaper.
